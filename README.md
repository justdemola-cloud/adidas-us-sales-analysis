# 📊 Adidas US Sales Analysis — Excel Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project is an **Excel-based analysis of Adidas US sales performance across 2020 and 2021**.

The analysis explores sales revenue, units sold, operating profit, operating margin, product performance, retailer performance, regional performance, sales methods, gender, and monthly sales trends.

The project transforms raw sales data into interactive dashboards that provide business insights and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

A large sales dataset can make it difficult to quickly understand which products, retailers, regions, and sales channels are driving business performance.

This project uses Microsoft Excel to analyze Adidas US sales data and identify:

- Sales and profitability trends.
- High-performing products.
- Strong-performing retailers.
- Regional performance differences.
- Sales-method distribution.
- Monthly sales patterns.
- Areas for potential business improvement.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall Adidas sales performance across 2020 and 2021.

### 5. Insights & Recommendations

The final dashboard summarizes the major findings from the analysis and translates them into business recommendations.

![Insights and Recommendations](Screenshot%202026-06-17%20085000.png)

#### Key Insights

**Strong growth in 2021**

Sales increased substantially from 2020 to 2021, with 2021 sales outperforming 2020 across the monthly comparison.

**West Gear's strong retail performance**

West Gear generated approximately **$137.55M** in the West region, significantly exceeding the performance of several other retailers in that region.

**Men's Street Footwear leads product sales**

Men's Street Footwear generated the highest total sales and units sold among the analyzed product categories.

**In-store sales remain significant**

The sales-method analysis shows a distribution of approximately:

- In-store: 40%
- Outlet: 33%
- Online: 27%

**Women's categories show strong demand**

Women's Apparel and Women's Street Footwear recorded substantial sales, indicating significant demand across women's product categories.

**Sales performance varies throughout the year**

The monthly analysis shows noticeable differences in sales performance across 2020 and 2021, providing opportunities for inventory and promotional planning.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations were identified:

- Investigate the factors behind the strong 2021 sales growth and use successful strategies as a benchmark for future planning.
- Strengthen partnerships with high-performing retailers such as West Gear.
- Review the performance of lower-performing retailers and investigate opportunities for improvement.
- Increase attention to growing women's product categories through inventory planning and targeted marketing.
- Continue strengthening in-store retail partnerships while improving the online customer experience.
- Plan inventory and promotional activities around periods of stronger and weaker sales performance.
- Use sales and profitability trends to support retailer, product, and regional strategy.
